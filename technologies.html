Core Technologies

The technological foundation that makes moral causality operational rather than rhetorical.

Technoneural Implants

A voluntary brain-computer interface you receive when you join. It's your ID, your safety system, your dashboard, and your connection to backup vessels. Your thoughts are completely private — only what you actually do affects your record.

  • Neural pattern scanning — distinguishes intent from thought
  • Real-time intent and execution detection with warnings
  • User-configurable failsafe motor inhibition
  • AR overlays and personal dashboards
  • STI ledger integration — dual-account system: the first track is the Social Trust Index score (continuous behavioral reliability metric), the second is the criminal record log (hard flags for qualifying offenses). Both tracks are persistent, non-erasable, and visible to institutional and private systems reading the ledger
  • Backup vessel sync and continuity anchoring
  • International passport — the implant serves as identity, citizenship proof, and behavioural record during international travel

Backup Vessels

Your mind is periodically backed up. If you die, you wake up in a new body with all your memories — but not a clean record. You're still the same person who did what you did. Revival either works perfectly or fails completely. No partial copies.

Revival reliability depends on your layer: nearly perfect in Sanctuary and Main (~1 in a million failure), lower in -1 (~1 in 10,000) and -2 (~1 in 1,000). In -3 Terminal, your backup link is permanently severed — death there is final.

"Backup vessels preserve continuity, not innocence."

Emergent Application

Food Synthesis: Fabrication proxy technology applied to molecularly simpler structures than human bodies. If VMSS can fabricate a backup vessel atom by atom, food production is a trivially solved subset — any cuisine, any ingredient, any nutritional profile, synthesized on demand without agriculture, supply chains, or animal farming.

Neural Diving

Experience the world through someone else's senses. Audience Mode lets you watch passively. Pilot Mode lets you temporarily inhabit someone else's perspective — with their consent, revocable at any moment. Used for education, therapy, empathy, and entertainment. All sessions are logged. Non-consensual use triggers immediate reassignment.

Emergent Applications

Full Sensory Media (ImmersionTube): Neural diving as a recording medium — capturing audio, vision, taste, touch, smell, proprioception, and emotional tone. A civilizational media platform distributing full sensory experiences. Every prior media format becomes partial.

Virtual Reality Without Hardware: Neural diving combined with AR overlays creates a holodeck without a room. The implant is the headset. Shared virtual spaces, AI-generated NPCs, full sensory immersion — no goggles, no controllers, no external hardware.

Transanimal Experiences: The civilian application of military neural diving animal-host technology. Experience flight through a hawk, ocean depth through a whale, or predator instinct through a wolf — audience mode only, fully consensual with the animal host.

Enforcement Systems

Medical drones arrive in seconds — not minutes. They stabilize injuries on-site and transport severe cases to hospitals. In Sanctuary, they also provide preventive and ongoing care.

Enforcement drones and turrets stop harmful acts mid-motion in Sanctuary and capture evidence in Main and below. AR surveillance (cameras, drones, biometrics) tracks identity regardless of whether someone has an implant — removing it doesn't erase your record.

The civilization aspires to cure every known ailment for every resident within institutional reach. In upper layers, medical coverage improves as technology advances. In lower layers, the cure may exist but access depends on private markets — that gap is by design.

Military Technologies — Classified

VMSS's military doesn't look like any military on Earth. Two weapons are publicly known. Everything else is classified.

Implant kill switch — instant, any scale, zero collateral damage. Works on anyone with an implant. Domestic defense only.

Nanobot weapons — for threats from people who don't have implants. Intelligent targeting, near-instant, each capsule can neutralize thousands. If you remove your implant to dodge the kill switch, you enter the nanobot system's range instead. There's no escape vector.

Biological Augmentation

Modify your body however you want — reversibly. Live 200–300 years (theoretically up to 1,000). Pin your appearance at any age. Change your features, your build, your species traits. Fertility extends to 500 years. Preview any modification through neural diving before committing.

Emergent Applications

Body as Canvas: Bioaugmentation as fashion and self-expression. Temporary bioluminescent skin patterns, structural hair and nail modifications, cosmetic trait shifts for events or seasons. Runway shows feature living models whose bodies are the medium — transformations performed live as performance art.

Bioengineered Companions: Pet dragons, miniature megafauna, novel organisms designed for temperament, size, and lifespan. Bioaugmentation applied to animal design — creating companion species that never existed in nature, engineered for cohabitation with human households.

Mega-Walls & Boundary Infrastructure

Massive walls separating the five rings — 15km above ground, 5km below ground, with a 1km-wide base tapering to a ~1m crest. Higher than commercial aircraft fly. Too deep to tunnel under. The kilometer-wide base makes them effectively immune to conventional breach. Packed with sensors, drones, and automated defenses.

Energy forcefields will be added on top of the physical walls in the 28th century. By 2850, the boundary is impenetrable by any known means.

Automated Labor & UBI Foundation

Robots and AI do most of the work. The surplus they generate funds everyone's basic income. In upper layers, automation handles 90%+ of production. In lower layers, there's more demand for human workers — creating a rawer, more competitive economy.
